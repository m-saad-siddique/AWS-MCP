MFA sessions
Reuse temporary AWS credentials until they expire, including after a restart.

      
Remember sessions on this Mac
Temporary credentials are saved in macOS Keychain. MFA codes are never saved. Saving a session does not extend its AWS expiration or change your approval policies.

        
        

        
Credentials remain on this machine and are never returned by session tools.

      

      

      
To start or renew a session, submit an AWS command and enter MFA in its approval dialog. SSO sessions are managed separately by AWS CLI. Forgetting a session removes its local cache; it does not revoke it at AWS or stop operations already running.
